# Exemplos autorais de utilização

Exemplos sintéticos planejados, sem imagem real observada nem vídeo renderizado. A descrição fornecida é a fonte; não alegar que suas propriedades vieram de pixels.

Pedido: “Cinco animações de uma pessoa com casaco cinza junto a uma janela, luz diurna lateral, sem fala”.

### 1. Cinematic Balanced
```text
Begin with a medium view of the person in the grey coat beside the window, preserving their appearance and the room layout. The camera makes a gentle dolly in as the subject takes a quiet breath and turns their gaze slightly toward the daylight. The coat settles naturally with the small body movement. Keep the side light stable, the eyes clearly focused and the background softly separated, ending on a calm closer framing in one continuous shot with no dialogue.
```

### 2. Cinematic Atmospheric
```text
Start from the same person in the grey coat beside the window, maintaining the original facial appearance, pose and room geometry. A slow lateral camera glide reveals subtle depth between the window frame and the subject as the person watches the daylight. Reflections on the glass shift gently with the viewpoint while the light direction remains consistent. Keep the air clear and the motion restrained, ending with the subject and window sharing the frame in a quiet continuous shot without speech.
```

### 3. Cinematic Dynamic
```text
Frame the person in the grey coat beside the window and preserve the face, wardrobe and setting. The camera performs a controlled shallow arc toward the front of the subject while the person turns their shoulders slightly to remain naturally oriented toward the window. Coat folds respond to the rotation and reflections change consistently with the camera. Maintain daylight from the same side, ending with a composed three-quarter view, no cuts and no dialogue.
```

### 4. Creative Poetic
```text
Open on the window reflection of the person in the grey coat while keeping their physical position and the room unchanged. A delicate focus transition moves from the reflected silhouette to the subject's eyes as the person lowers their gaze for a moment and then looks toward the light. The camera stays nearly still, allowing the emotional change and layered glass reflection to carry the scene. Preserve natural facial identity and stable side illumination, ending in a contemplative continuous shot without dialogue.
```

### 5. Creative High Impact
```text
Begin with a wider view of the person in the grey coat beside the window, preserving the subject and room proportions. Use a brief controlled dolly in that accelerates gently and then decelerates before reaching a close medium framing. The subject raises their gaze toward the lens with a clear but natural expression as the coat moves subtly with the posture change. Keep the face readable, daylight consistent and camera path unobstructed. Finish on a stable composition without cuts, added objects or dialogue.
```

## Revisão de conflito
Entrada: “Camera Static e 360 Orbit ao mesmo tempo; único plano.”
Saída corrigida:
```text
Begin with a static medium shot to establish the subject and environment, then transition smoothly into a restrained partial orbit along a clear unobstructed path. Keep the subject's identity, wardrobe and spatial position consistent, maintain the same motivated lighting and settle into a stable final framing within one continuous shot.
```
Explicação: a câmera fica estática primeiro e depois se move; não se exigem estados incompatíveis simultaneamente. Se 360 graus completos forem obrigatórios, adaptar duração e espaço em vez de prometer resultado impossível.
